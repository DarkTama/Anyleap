Dragon-Scale Leap (B1 Refined) — Ink, Lifted Slate & Gold · Strict Flat System

Design Brief & Refinements

Cross-device input & display workstation linking phone and desktop seamlessly. Isometric 30° geometry with square symbol footprint (w:h = 1.00).

  • 16 px Small Cut: Dragon scales merge into one continuous gold stroke with 2 clean breaks.
  • Dual-Gold Contrast: #D8B46A on #1B1E2E navy (8.38:1); #A9822F deep gold on white (3.55:1).
  • Lifted 3-Tone Slate: #6A7196 / #4F5575 / #3B3F59 clearly separate from the #1B1E2E tile.
  • Strict Flat Spec: Zero gradients, zero drop shadows, zero glows across every asset.

Clear Space & Minimums
• Clear space: ≥ 1/4 symbol height on all sides.
• Full symbol min: 32 px.
• Small cut: <32 px (16–31 px).
• Lockup min height: 24 px.
• No gradients, shadows, or glows.
